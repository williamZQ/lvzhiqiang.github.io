Biography

Zhiqiang Lv is currently a Young Talent at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Qingdao University of Technology, and concurrently serves as a Senior AI Consultant at the R&D Center of PerfXLab. His research focuses on Intelligent Transportation Systems, Spatio-temporal Artificial Intelligence, Spatio-temporal Large Language Models, and High-Performance Computing. He is also the proposer and theoretical founder of the Spatio-temporal Tree Neural Network. Proficient in AI, big data, deep learning, and computer vision technologies, he has published over 60 academic papers in journals such as IEEE Transactions.
